[[Happiness]] too have depth, as light hearted we become in the moment of happiness, so vulnerable we are in those time.
This depth is neither any thinking, nor philosophical, every individual carry within them, in the cave of their heart, say the sages, the POTENTIAL.

A potential to feel true happiness, the one which penetrates farther, and deeper than the layers of death and suffering, it is so self-fulfilled despite of all dualities of life, naturally by itself, addictively obsessive, just like Radha-Krishna was to each other, and yet it's texture is so dangerous and trustful simultaneously.

A happiness which is not a fruit hanging at some distance, and we have to chase it, but rather our own identity, we have to re-claim it.

This unnamed quality of ourselves makes this life worth living till the last breath, every inhalation and exhalation feels like an incomprehensible intimacy with an unknown lover.

Human capacity to feel this existence is immeasurable. And no human is so special that the one is deprived from this gift. 

But what price is it that we have to pay to be this happy ?
or perhaps I must ask
What price is it that we have to pay to love this deeply ?

~Srinivasayy

( Series 1.7, Intuitive Communication with SELF)
