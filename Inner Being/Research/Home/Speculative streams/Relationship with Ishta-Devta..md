[[Yugala Consciousness]] - [[non-dual]]


------------

Note : 

This is from [[non-dual]] perspective, where you are the attribute/ mode of supreme brahman as your Ishta devta.

They are the Para-rupa and you are Anu-rupa.
They are the Sariri and you are Sarira.
They are the Praskha and you are Vimarsha.

Your Ishta can be He/She/They as per your selection.

And from Rasa-leela perspective of non-duality, you become the bhava/movement/ flow of love in the heart of your Ishta.
You become their sense of love/ devotion.


From Yugal Perspective :

- God is not the object of devotion
- You are not the subject of devotion

**Devotion itself becomes conscious.**

That is what it means to stand  
_between_ the Yugal not as a third,  

but as the **living sweetness of their unity**.
