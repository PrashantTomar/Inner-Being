Note : 

From 3.22 of Bhagwat Gita :

> न मे पार्थास्ति कर्तव्यं त्रिषु लोकेषु किञ्चन । नानवाप्तमवाप्तव्यं वर्त एव च कर्मणि ॥ २२ ॥

**Transliteration:** _na me pārthāsti kartavyaṁ triṣu lokeṣu kiñcana |_ _nānavāptam avāptavyaṁ varta eva ca karmaṇi || 22 ||_

---

### Translation

**"O son of Pritha (Arjuna), in the three worlds, there is no duty for Me to perform, nor is there anything for Me to attain that I have not already attained. Yet, I continue to be engaged in prescribed duties."**
