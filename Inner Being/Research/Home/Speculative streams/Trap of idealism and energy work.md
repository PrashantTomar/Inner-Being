Note : 

One can immediately stumble with wrong steps from any direction when one approaches to spiritually attune oneself with TRUTH.


Sometimes we fall into the trap of philosophical ideals, which looks like far away from who we are and what we 'seems' to want from life, bestowing complete dissonance between our personal actions, our chosen ideal and social world, mixture of what corresponds to our so-called personal life.

Another is the trap of energy work, where we just dwell into the depth of psyche without even bother about the knowledge of nature of things, out of greed, or ignorance. Here people just don't care of GOD much, there is just supernaturality in the name of spirituality and all the power-games.

And what we truly want is balance or harmony between both, only then attunement to our higher SELF becomes smoother and deeply fulfilling in complete sense.
