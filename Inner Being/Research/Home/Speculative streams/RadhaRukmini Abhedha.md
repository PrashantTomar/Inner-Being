Note : 
Esoteric meaning of [[Radha]] and [[Rukmini]] :

>Rukmini dwaravatyam Ca Radha vrindavana vane ( That which is Rukmini in Dwarka is Radha in Vrindavan)
>~ Matsya Purana


Vrindavan and Dwarka are like two realm where one's ground state revolve around anahad chakra and ones in manipura-anahad chakra, respectively. 

Devotees in accordance with their tendencies/ karmic lessons might bend towards any one these, but in the eyes of Lord [[Krishna]], Vrindavan and Dwarika is same and one essentially.
