Note : 

Yoga is to be in tune with truth. Yoga is anything which reveals or reflects the wholeness that we truly are, and the ‘world’ is anything that makes us feel that we are fragmented, divided and out of tune with ourselves.

“Balance is yoga,” (Samatvam yoga uchyate.), says the Srimad Bhagavad Gita. A balance of forces is yoga, or simply, balance is yoga. Harmony is yoga; imbalance is not yoga. Imbalance is being out of tune with oneself. To be in tune with oneself is yoga. To practise yoga one need not necessarily leave the world, nor does it mean going here and there to this ashram or that ashram. All these things are not yoga.
