Note : 

If any artha bhavam of your has brings you closer towards god, if that artha bhavam had awaken pure presence of GOD within you and everyones heart simultaenously, then that artha bhavam is same as any artha bhavam for lord, irrespective of any situational pain/suffering ones goes into.

This comes from 9.26 of Bhagwat Gita.
The artha bhavam has such a glory, as depicted in Gajendra Moksha.


That artha bhavam is GOD himself, and so deeply personal, it can't be trapped into the mere cage of reasoning. Hence one should avoid articulating and sharing ones sharnagata and artha bhavam to others, as that which should have been received with deep sense of humility and vulnerability will just be subject random logic and reason, which isn't anyways fruitful for anyone.

So better keep it quiet and enjoy your personal intimacy with god.

And instead focus on prayer for Lokha samastu sukhino bhavantu.

Further question arises.

Q: So if artha bhavam is GOD himself, why should we protect it, it is indestructible, maybe 10000 people will disrespect it, but there will be 1 for whom it will matter the most, and that is enough for me.

A : You can't educate someone into the experiential "taste" of innocence.
Secondly my any articulation of this bhavam will incomplete, it would never be justified.
Thirdly, there is difference between educating around truth and justifying your depth for truth.

Your life lived is the only proof for others, not your articulations/claims around justification of your love or artha bhavam or innocence.

but for lord your bhavam is enough, just direct perception.
