Remembering the Importance of Meditation :

Unless we are able to convince the mind in regard to the necessity for such a concentration as a part of discipline of spiritual life, there would be no success in meditation. We should be a hundred percent honest and sincere when we embark upon this adventure, as I mentioned. We are not just joking with ourselves. The art of meditation is not a humour, or a kind of diversion. We are not merely making ourselves feel that we are doing something worthwhile for ourselves just because we have nothing else to do. It is nothing of the kind. It is a life-and-death matter for us. Spiritual meditation is a matter of life and death. It is not an ordinary joke, because it is that which is going to decide what we are going to be in our future life. Not merely that, it will decide what kind of success we can have in this life itself. The extent of success in any walk of life here in this life, and the nature of our future in another life, will all be decided by the extent of our success in meditation. Meditation is a glorious adventure. It is a magnificent endeavour. The moment the mind thinks of the glory of meditation, it will be in a state of rapture. It will be possessed by a power which is beyond itself because of the completeness which the object of meditation represents.

Remembering the importance of Sitting/ Standing/ Asana tattva :

It’s a mark of spiritual humility, that we cannot jump directly to apex _always_ and then sustain it, it helps to realize the highest in the ground. Stabilizes the psyche and makes us receptive to _LIGHT_ with the stability of Earth.

Remembering the finality of our method of concentration :

Where and how are we going to concentrate on this must be very clear to us, is it inside, outside, an abstract symbol, a yantra, a trataka, get clarity over it and then we are not suppose to change it.

Remembering the finality of our philosophy :

Once one get’s inner spiritual sense of high principles of life, by grace and longing to know, an appropriate philosophy comes to that individual.

Most appropriate philosophical development for an individual is a matter of great grace, choose the way you want relate with higher principles of life, anyways everything at the end of the day boils downs to ones ability to devote to love, but philosophy of tattva makes mind eventually non-wavering and helps to establish in truth and devotion.

Remembering the finality of our Ishta-Devata and Core Source of their Study :

After analysing the core attribute of Devta/ Deity, the one who resemble closely with us, one who becomes star of our eyes, is finally our ISHTA, with whom we can give our everything, with whom we can believe that our inner structure can truly become possessed with, and we can fully surrender to them, with whom we can tune our daily life, and find gladness in their swa-bhava.

The finality of our sense of belonging and refuge.

Pick their Sahasranama, Strotam to your taste, and Nama-Mantra, and study it your whole life, It will be enough, more than enough.

Remembering the Nijj Seva-Tattvam for individual personal duty :

The way we personally like to do service of lord, the skills we would like to develop to express our life, the energies we would like to refine within ourselves, for the sake of service.

There are people who consciously choose a life of poor and beggar, maybe that’s their style of giving service to lord.

It’s more about being true to your expression and it’s refinement.

How one’s personal duty has different level ?

From general task of managing oneself, one’s home, one’s relationships to refining skills to produce body of ‘work’.

Foundation of Yoga :

Yama-Niyama-Asana

Yama-Niyama is the analysis of your heart and your feelings. Where is your heart; where is your feeling? What is it that you are attracted to?

Yama is how we relate world outside/ society with the inner glory of SELF.

Niyama is how we conduct ourselves in a manner that our sense of yama keeps refining into higher principles.

Asana is fixity of our attitude, such that it supports Yama-Niyama dynamics, and establishes in us the glory of love.
