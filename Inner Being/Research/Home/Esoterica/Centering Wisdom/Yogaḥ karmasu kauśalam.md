Note : 

>buddhiyukto jahātīha ubhe sukṛtaduṣkṛte
  tasmādyogāya yujyasva yogaḥ karmasu kauśalam
  
  >**Meaning:** One who is endowed with wisdom (buddhi-yoga) discards both good and evil deeds in this world. Therefore, devote yourself to yoga, for yoga is skill in action.

This verse emphasizes that true yoga is not just physical practice, but the art of performing actions skillfully, with detachment and balance.

Yoga as skill in action.
