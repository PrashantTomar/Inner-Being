Daiva, Manushya and Asura are states of mind, as All is RAM anyways.


these are trichotomized on the level of their intensities.

Daiva :

those who seek deeper state of relationship with their ishta-devta, which verily is the embodiment of absolute truth.

Asura :

those who don't seek deeper state of relationship with their ishta-devta, and or totally unbothered with idea of truth or self-honesty and infact rather go in opposite direction, causing violence to innocence, through their personal conduct.

Manushya :

these are people who are on neutral plane, which is to say that, their ishta-devta is not a highest priority in their life and they lives on the edge of daiva and asura, but their personal conduct is non-harming to society or their community.
