Note : 

Strotam are treated as high-quality psychological kindness we do to SELF, they by themselves are crafted and charged with qualitative sense of devotion, emotional rasa and knowledge around supreme reality and your deity.

When chanted along mantras, they form a beautiful catalyst, and helps devotee to create quick relatability with their deity.

Different strotams are alive and they have their own subtle body, as per the body of meaning of strota.

Strotam + Mantra -> Quicker healing.

Sahasranama + Strotam + Mantra -> Best of all.


>_Stavya-stava-priya stotram stuta stotaa rana priyah |_
_Poornah poorayita punyah punya-keerti rana-mayah_  

from Vishnu Sahasranama - 73 shloka

Stavya = he is being praised and worshipped by all

Stavya priya = he likes being praised and worshipped

Stotram = a glorious hymn that describes Lord Vishnu’s nature

Stutih = the act of chanting stotra

Stoota = the devotee who chants the stotra

- ( the object, the act and the performer all three are Lord Vishnu)

Rana priya = Rana is war, he likes war

Poorna = he is complete

Poorayitah = he looks after those who worship him

Punya = the pure and holy

Punya keerthi = holy fame

Anamaya = he is untouched by physical or emotional weakness.
